## The List Includes:

  - **Budget Kings:** AULA 75 & AULA S75 Pro.
  - **The Legend:** Rainy 75 Pro (The benchmark for aluminum boards).
  - **Low Profile:** NuPhy Air75 V3 & LOFREE 85.
  - **Premium:** EVOWORKS Evo75.
  - **Unique:** NuPhy Kick75 & Melgeek O2.

## Selection Criteria:

  - **Pleasant Sound:** Must be "Thocky" or "Creamy" out of the box.
  - **Customization:** Hotswap support (switches/keycaps).
  - **Connectivity:** Bluetooth, RF (USB dongle), Wired.
  - **Aesthetics:** A cool-looking case is the priority.
