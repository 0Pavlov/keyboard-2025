Choosing the Perfect Keyboard (by my opinion)

Comparison of contenders, analysis of pros, cons, and potential for modding.

Selection Criteria

  • Pleasant sound ("Thocky" / "Creamy")
  • Potential for customization later (Hotswap):
    • Swappable switches (keygeek y2 / y3)
    • Decent / large selection of keycaps
  • Support for different connection types:
    • Bluetooth, RF (USB dongle), Wired.
  • Presence of F-row
  • Cool looking case: the only thing I don't want to have to change later.

AULA 75 ~$50

▶ Sound out of box (youtube) ▶ Sound after mod (youtube)

Pros

  • Cool looking case
  • Has a volume roller (feels cheap)
  • Cheap price
  • Customizable (community choice for custom under $100)
  • Decent colorway out of the box (keycaps)

Cons

  • Plastic case
  • Requires modding immediately (switches, lube)
  • Big
  • Stock keycaps are not shine-through (don't glow in dark)
  • No screws for disassembly (case needs prying, risk of damage)
Conclusion: OK out of the box, cheap, can be customized, big.

AULA S75 Pro ~$60

▶ Sound out of box (youtube) ▶ Sound after mod (youtube)

Pros

  • Volume roller (Good)
  • Case looks better than basic AULA version
  • Customizable
  • Decent colorway
  • Has a screen
  • Newer model
  • Cheap

Cons

  • Has a screen (aesthetically prefer basic AULA)
  • Requires modding immediately (switches, lube)
  • Big
  • Stock keycaps are not shine-through (don't glow in dark)
  • Plastic case
Conclusion: OK out of the box, cheap, can be customized, big, ugly screen.

Royal Kludge M75

▶ Sound out of box (youtube) ▶ Sound after mod can be like AULA (youtube)

Pros

  • Looks better than AULA PRO (screen isn't cringe)
  • Higher quality volume roller
  • Direct AULA competitor that surpasses it

Cons

  • Hard to disassemble (same as AULA)
  • Plastic
  • Twice the price of AULA, but still a budget board (no aluminum)
Conclusion: Take this instead of AULA if you like this form factor.

NuPhy Air75 V3

▶ Sound out of box (youtube) ▶ Customization options (youtube)

Pros

  • Looks decent out of the box (almost boring)
  • Has a volume roller (3 swappable ones included)
  • Typing sound is great out of the box (thock / creamy)
  • Option with silent switches included
  • Comes with cool keycaps featuring Google colors and a cat
  • Low profile, slim, easily fits in a backpack
  • Aluminum body (feels premium)

Cons

  • Almost not customizable:
    • Shine-through keycaps cost $35
    • Only Low Profile switches
  • Stock keycaps are not shine-through (don't glow in dark)
Conclusion: Good out of the box, stays as is forever, slim.

NuPhy Kick75

▶ Sound out of box (youtube)

Pros

  • Unique retro look / Nike shoe style
  • Has a volume roller
  • Good "thocky" sound out of the box
  • Available in low and high profile versions
  • Converts from low to high (kit for $20)
  • Supports custom switches (but only their Low Profile ones)

Cons

  • Design is an "acquired taste" (specific), keyboard will look a certain way even after customization
  • NuPhy Ecosystem (only their LP switches, conversion kit, and LP keycaps)
  • THEIR CUSTOM LOW PROFILE KEYCAPS COST $25-30 EACH (ROBBERY)
  • Plastic case, looks toy-ish
Conclusion: Unique look (might get bored of it, which is hard to fix), good sound, low customization.

Rainy 75 Pro The Legend

▶ Sound out of box (youtube) ▶ Sound after mods can be anything (youtube)

Pros

  • Absolutely gorgeous case (CNC aluminum) with a beautiful bottom plate and a cute cloud on the front
  • Looks decent out of the box (stock keycaps)
  • Decent sound out of the box
  • Highest customizability (community choice 1-2 years ago)

Cons

  • Really old model (can affect software, battery, connection quality)
  • Ugly red PCB visible under the keys
Conclusion: Beautiful, requires customization immediately (switches, lube, possibly keycaps), big, old tech.

LOFREE 85

▶ Sound out of box (youtube)

Pros

  • Minimalism, no bloat
  • Decent sound out of the box
  • Low profile, slim, easily fits in a backpack
  • Cool desk illumination from the bottom of the keyboard
  • Aluminum body (feels premium)
  • Decent keycaps with shine-through symbols

Cons

  • Strange insert with LOFREE logo on the side
  • Questionable bottom
  • Almost not customizable:
    • Stock keycaps look strange, almost cheap
    • Only Low Profile switches fit
Conclusion: Solid variant "as is", slim, low profile, design is okay.

EVOWORKS Evo75 Premium

▶ Sound out of box (youtube)

Pros

  • Beautiful Full-Aluminum case
  • Cool "thocky" sound out of the box (Rye switches and PP plate variant)
  • Very cool design with a nice LED near Esc
  • Cool joystick on the right
  • High customizability

Cons

  • The most expensive option of all
  • Likely need to replace switches with Keygeek v2 / v3 or other thocky ones
  • Requires lubrication and stabilizer work
  • Heaviest (2kg)
Conclusion: Most premium variant with minor cons, expensive, very good "non-screaming" design, so can be customized in many ways, refreshing the look over time.

PMO wave 75

▶ Sound out of box (youtube)

Pros

  • Basically a modern version of Rainy75
  • Backlit logo on the right (Rainy75 cloud doesn't light up)
  • High customizability
  • Minimalist design, so keyboard look is heavily impacted by different keycaps
  • Battery and connectivity better than Rainy because it's newer

Cons

  • No "masterpiece" on the bottom like Rainy75 Pro
  • Needs switch replacement right out of the box
  • Heavy
Conclusion: Direct competitor to Rainy, with more modern internals, backlit accent on the front, but no masterpiece on the bottom.

Melgeek O2

▶ Sound out of box (youtube)

Pros

  • Minimalist design (my favorite among Low Profile)
  • Low profile
  • "Creamy" sound (mix of thock and cream, pretty quiet)
  • Cool LED on the right
  • Lightweight
  • Really good keycaps out of the box
  • Additional keycaps with bees

Cons

  • No shine-through keycaps
  • Plastic case (not really a con in this case)
  • No customization
  • Not disassemble-able at all
Conclusion: Really great choice for a low profile keyboard to use "as is" without customization — solid.
